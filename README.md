### the game base:
#### You live in A Camper (like the VW Bulli T1), That you can customize ect. So this is basically ur home screen. The main goal is to collect flowers and put them into your herbarium. You can travel through the woods the mountains, grass lands and villages there you can trade flowers and talk to the villagers to get missions, information or other stuff. Or you can sell flowers to buy decorations for your bus.
